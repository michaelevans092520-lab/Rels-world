# Originality & IP rules (read before adding ANYTHING)

Rel's World is a **commercial game that the owner plans to sell**. Every piece that ships must be
original and provably owned, so nothing can be flagged, claimed, or struck later.
These rules apply to every chat, every file, and every asset.

## Code
- Write everything from scratch with standard, generic patterns. Never copy or closely mimic code,
  formulas, algorithms, data tables or architectures from existing games (including OSRS/RuneScape,
  WoW, MapleStory), tutorials, Stack Overflow answers or public repos.
- Gameplay rules come from the owner's written specs. Don't invent mechanics; ask about missing details.
- No libraries or packages without the owner's approval. Before asking, state the license and any
  commercial limits. Currently approved: TypeScript compiler (Apache-2.0, dev tool only, not shipped).
- **The old browser prototype (root `index.html` + `js/`, and the `prototype` branch) must never ship
  and must never be copied into `server/`.** Parts of it were modeled on OSRS.

## Art, models, animation, music, sound, fonts
- Final game: **only commissioned or owner-made work, with a signed agreement that assigns all rights
  to the owner** (work-for-hire / IP assignment).
- Store assets, free CC0 packs and AI-generated assets may be used **only as temporary placeholders**,
  logged in `IP-LOG.md`, and replaced before release.
- Never use anything ripped from another game, even as a placeholder.
- Fonts must allow commercial use and redistribution (or be custom).

## Names
- Don't use names, terms or skill names recognizable from other games.
- Trademark-check the game title and major names before release.

## Records
- Log every asset in `docs/IP-LOG.md`: what it is, who made it, the license or contract, the date,
  and whether it's final or a placeholder.
- Keep the owner's design specs in the repo. They are evidence of human authorship.

Not legal advice: an IP attorney should review contracts and the log before any sale or release.
