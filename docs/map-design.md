# Map design notes

Status: **draft from the prototype era.** The real game's map hasn't been specced yet. Region names
are placeholders (**Country 1–8**) until the owner picks final names.

## Plan: one connected supercontinent ("Pangea") first
- Every country is joined and walkable, with no boats or loading screens.
- Each country has its own look, monsters, level range and fighting style, so it still *feels* like a separate land.
- Borders are natural transitions with a few road chokepoints, so the map could be split into islands later.

## The 8 countries
Numbered in level order. Country 1 is the planned starter area (not yet confirmed for the real game).

| Country | Levels | Known for | Look | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Country 1 | 1-10 | Swordsmanship | Grassland, village, dark forest, south beaches and dock | South centre (start) |
| Country 2 | 10-20 | Archers | Cobblestone city | Centre |
| Country 3 | 20-30 | Earth magic* | Crystal flats and sand | South-west |
| Country 4 | 30-40 | Necromancy | Swamp, bog pools, dead trees | West |
| Country 5 | 40-50 | Strength warriors | Snow, pine forests | North-west |
| Country 6 | 50-65 | Fire magic* | Ash and lava | North-east |
| Country 7 | 65-80 | Water magic* | Coral sand, lagoons, palms | East |
| Country 8 | 80-99 | Air & star magic | Star-grass, glowing trees (endgame) | North centre |

\* These were called "benders" earlier. **Don't use "bender" in the real game.** That word is strongly tied to an existing franchise. "Magic" is a neutral placeholder until the owner names these styles.

Roads (prototype layout): 1 → 2 → 8; 2 → 7 → 6; 1 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 8 → 6.

Nothing is locked: higher-level countries are simply more dangerous. A banner warns you when you enter a country above your level.

## Later (maybe): break the supercontinent apart
- Possibly split it into separate islands down the line (story event, or just how the map evolves).
- Each country is its own chunk with clear borders, so road chokepoints could become docks or boat routes.
